How to play

Habitats – manage up to 8 enclosure slots. Different enclosure types have different dimensions, capacities, decor catalogs and layout presets. Use the Tank tab at the top to switch enclosures; choose Manage Habitats there to buy, rename or replace enclosures.

Decor / Plants / Substrate / Presets – each enclosure has its own compatible decor catalog and adaptive layout presets. Pick an item and click inside the tank to place it. R rotates. Esc cancels. Save your own layouts from Presets and reuse them in compatible habitats. Remove can remove decor, prey, jumpers, remains, droplets or silk; decor refunds 50%.

Live Food – pick prey and click in the tank to drop it in. Springtails act as cleanup fauna rather than hunting targets; dwarf isopods, waxworms and darkling beetles add different movement and hiding behavior. Flowers attract flies & moths. Moths love the night.

Jumpers – unlock species with coins and place them. Click a jumper to see what it is thinking. Individuals develop different habits, memories and personalities; species also have stronger identities such as wall-running, canopy hunting, display behavior or tactical ambushing. Age and molt history now affect life stage and movement.

Jumpers spot prey, plan routes, close distance quickly when prey is far, slow into a careful creep near striking range, freeze when watched from farther away, and commit to a pounce if prey gets too close. Jump reach varies by jumper. Other jumpers can notice, threaten, flee, or become prey themselves; larger and more mature jumpers dominate cannibalistic encounters.

Cameras – keys 1 isometric, 2 Observer, 3 Follow, 4 Reverse. Observation Mode (📹 or O) hides the management UI and follows interesting behavior automatically. Zoom with the mouse wheel, pinch, or the +/− buttons; drag to pan when zoomed in. On phones, the primary actions stay along the bottom and More opens the remaining controls. Mist makes droplets they drink. Progress saves automatically.
