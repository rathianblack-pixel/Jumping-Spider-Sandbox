How to play

3 tanks – switch with the tabs at the top. Each can hold up to 3 jumpers.

Decor / Plants / Substrate – pick an item, click inside the tank to place it. R rotates. Esc cancels. Remove lets you click decor to pick it up (50% refund).

Live Food – pick prey and click in the tank to drop it in. Flowers attract flies & moths. Moths love the night.

Jumpers – unlock species with coins and place them. Click a jumper to see what it's thinking. Hover near one and it will watch you.

Jumpers spot prey, plan a route (often climbing to attack from above or behind), freeze when watched, line up, then pounce on a silk safety line. Prey can notice and flee.

Cameras – keys 1 isometric, 2 side, 3 close-up follow cam, 4 back view (see behind the decor). Zoom with the mouse wheel, pinch, or the +/− buttons; drag to pan when zoomed in. On touch screens, drag to aim decor and lift your finger to place it. Mist makes droplets they drink. Jumpers grow and molt as they eat. Progress saves automatically.

⚙ Settings

Changing render quality reloads the game (progress is saved).
